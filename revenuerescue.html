Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Revenue Rescue

Transparent, zero-risk recovery protocols engineered for high-growth SaaS teams.

How does Revenue Rescue integrate with my Stripe account? +

Integration takes less than 60 seconds via Stripe's official OAuth or Read-Only Restricted API Keys (rk_live_...). We only request read access to invoice telemetry and subscription status. We never hold your customer funds.

What happens if you don't recover any revenue? +

You pay strictly $0. Our model is 100% pay-on-success: we take a capped 10% commission on recovered cash that would have otherwise churned, with a strict monthly cap of $39/mo (or 25,000 FCFA). If we don't save you money, you owe nothing.

How does the Zero-Auth 1-Tap Dunning link work for customers? +

When a recurring payment fails, your customer receives an encrypted, time-limited magic link via SMS/Email. They can instantly update their card or authorize payment via Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Mobile Money without having to remember passwords or log in.

Is Revenue Rescue PCI-DSS and SOC-2 compliant? +

Yes. All payment credentials are processed directly through Stripe's certified PCI-DSS Level 1 vault. We use cryptographic HMAC tokens and never store full card numbers or sensitive banking data.
